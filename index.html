Concept brief · Live on your website

Intelligent Client Portal

A simple, branded space on your website where new and existing clients start and follow their matter — while the system does the chasing, summarising, and updating for your solicitors.

Built for Family, Immigration, and Tort — as a client companion alongside LEAP / Clio, not a replacement for your team.

What this portal achieves

For clients
  • One trusted place for forms, documents, updates, and messages
  • No more hunting through Hotmail / Gmail for “where is my case?”
  • Works for overseas clients outside UK office hours
  • Clear next steps — what we need from them, and when
For solicitors
  • AI short summary of intake + documents before you open the full pack
  • Know intent and gaps in minutes, not after reading everything
  • Fewer “any update?” calls — especially from HNW clients
  • Document chasing happens automatically, with a full history
For the firm
  • Lives on your website — easy for anyone to find and use
  • Consistent intake quality across Immigration, Family, Tort
  • Official email + portal stay in sync and trackable
  • Human support still available when clients need a person

What gets automated vs what saves solicitor time

Automated by the portal
  • Guided intake forms by practice area (branching questions)
  • Document upload checklist for each case type
  • AI case summary (few lines) + document overview for the solicitor
  • Instant acknowledgment to client (portal + official email)
  • Reminders if requested docs are not received (e.g. after 3 days, then again)
  • Timeline logging: who was reminded, when, how many times
  • Status updates visible to the client when you mark a stage
  • Payment prompts / confirmation hand-off into messaging
Time you get back
  • No re-reading long unstructured emails to understand a new matter
  • No manually chasing missing passports, statements, certificates
  • No repeating the same status over the phone
  • Incomplete files filtered before they hit your desk
  • You focus on advice, strategy, and sensitive conversations
  • Clear audit: e.g. “Amy received follow-up reminder 4 times on these dates”
Live on your website

Clients open your site → choose New client or Existing client → the portal guides them. No app install. Mobile-friendly. Same brand, same trust — with automation behind the scenes.

How it works — two paths

Click a path below. Each includes a real example so you can see the journey end to end.

Example — New immigration client

Someone visits your website wanting help with a spouse visa extension. They have never instructed you before. Instead of emailing a long story and attachments, they open the portal and follow a short, clear path.

New client workflow

1
Client · Website

Opens your site → New client → selects Immigration.

2
Client · Smart form

Answers the questions a solicitor actually needs: who they are, visa type, immigration history, dependants, leave expiry / urgency, sponsorship details if relevant. Follow-up questions adapt (so they are not asked irrelevant things).

Automated · branching intake
3
Client · Documents

Uploads what the matter needs — passport pages, Home Office letters, relationship evidence, bank statements, any forms you require. Checklist shows what is still missing.

Automated · document checklist
4
Portal · AI brief

On submit, the solicitor receives a few-line AI summary: intent of the case, key facts, urgency, and obvious gaps — plus an indexed list of documents — without reading every page first.

Automated · AI summary for solicitor
5
Portal + official email

Client gets an immediate acknowledgment: we have received your enquiry; a solicitor will review; here is what happens next. Everything is logged and trackable.

Automated · acknowledgment
6
Solicitor

Reads the short brief, understands intent in minutes, replies with confirmation, any extra documents needed, and payment / engagement next steps — via portal (and email). Client sees it in one thread.

7
Both sides

Once the case is accepted and payment is done, messaging continues in the portal. Status, documents, and history stay in one place — not scattered across inboxes.

What does the solicitor see in the AI summary? (sample)

“Applicant seeks spouse visa extension; in UK; British partner. Leave expires in ~9 weeks. No refusals disclosed. Relationship evidence uploaded. Gaps: last 6 months’ bank statements and English language certificate missing. Suggested next step: acknowledge, request gaps, send engagement + fee link.”

Same idea for Family or Tort?

Yes — different question packs and document lists. Family can branch on sensitive points (e.g. whether a partner knows about a divorce). Tort focuses on incident details, limitation risk, and evidence. The workflow shape stays the same.

Example — Existing family client (“Amy”)

Amy already has a matter with you. She needs to send a document the solicitor asked for, and she wants to know what is happening — without calling the office or searching old emails.

Existing client workflow

1
Client · Website

Opens your site → Existing client → enters client / matter number and verifies (e.g. secure code).

2
Client · Dashboard

Sees case status in plain language, outstanding tasks, shared documents, messages, and payments — a single reference point for a long-running matter.

3
Solicitor · Request

Solicitor asks for an additional document (e.g. marriage certificate) through the portal. Amy is notified in the portal and by official email.

4
Portal · Reminders

If nothing is received after 3 days, a reminder goes straight to Amy. Reminders can repeat on a set schedule. The portal timeline records every chase — e.g. “Amy received follow-up reminder 4 times on [dates].”

Automated · chase reminders + history
5
Client · Upload

Amy uploads the document when ready. Completeness updates. Solicitor is notified that the file is ready to review again.

Automated · ready-for-review signal
6
Both sides · Ongoing

Updates, court papers you choose to share, emails of record, and messages stay in the portal. Overseas clients check progress in their own time zone. Sensitive matters can still request a call with a person.

Please upload it via your client portal when you can, so we can keep your matter moving without delay.

Upload document in portal

Reminder 1 of a scheduled follow-up · Also visible on your portal timeline. If you have already uploaded this, you can ignore this message.

[Solicitor Name]
[Your Firm Name]

Sample timeline Amy (and the solicitor) would see

12 Mar — Solicitor requested marriage certificate · email + portal notice sent
15 Mar — Automated reminder #1
18 Mar — Automated reminder #2
21 Mar — Automated reminder #3
24 Mar — Automated reminder #4
25 Mar — Amy uploaded marriage certificate · matter marked ready for review

Why this reduces office noise

Status-check calls drop because the answer is already on the dashboard. Solicitors stop being the reminder system. History is visible if anyone asks “did we chase them?” — yes, and here’s when.

Why this is easy to use — and highly automatable

Easy for clients

  • Found on your website — no new app to install
  • Two clear buttons: new or existing
  • Guided steps; only relevant questions
  • Works on phone; helpful for clients abroad
  • “Speak to someone” still available when needed

Automatable for the firm

  • Intake → AI brief → acknowledge → chase → update
  • Rules you set once (e.g. remind after 3 days)
  • Sits beside LEAP / Clio as the client-facing layer
  • Email and portal stay aligned and auditable
  • You only step in for advice and decisions

Note on “real time” (Home Office / sponsor licence)

Clients can see your matter progress as soon as your team updates it. Live Home Office application status depends on what integrations are actually available — we should confirm that before promising it. Firm-managed timelines (e.g. a ~10 working-day sponsor licence window) can still be shown clearly in the portal.
